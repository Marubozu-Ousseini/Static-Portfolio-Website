AI & Cloud Consulting FAQ

Direct answers for teams evaluating AI readiness, cloud modernization, and UAE/GCC compliance work.

What does Ousseini Oumarou do?

Ousseini Oumarou is an AI & Cloud Consultant who helps engineering teams and business owners design compliant, cost-efficient cloud and AI systems across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

Who do you work with?

I work remotely with teams worldwide and consult for private-sector companies in the UAE and GCC that need practical AI adoption, cloud architecture, PDPL-aware data governance, and cost control.

What is an AI readiness sprint?

An AI readiness sprint reviews workflows, data quality, compliance exposure, human approval points, cloud cost drivers, and outcome metrics before a company invests in AI implementation.

Why does UAE/GCC compliance matter for AI?

AI systems often process customer, operational, and business-sensitive data. In the UAE and GCC, PDPL, data residency, sovereign cloud expectations, and auditability can shape architecture choices from day one.

Which cloud platforms do you support?

I work across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, with strong AWS experience in Lambda, API Gateway, S3, DynamoDB, Bedrock, serverless architecture, and secure production operations.

How quickly can a team see value?

The goal is to move from strategy to impact in weeks, not years: identify one realistic use case, validate data readiness, define controls, and create a delivery roadmap before scaling.
